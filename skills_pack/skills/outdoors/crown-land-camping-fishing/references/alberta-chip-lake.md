# Chip Lake, Alberta — Verified Coordinates (2026 session correction)

## CRITICAL: real lake location
- Chip Lake center (OpenStreetMap-verified): **53.6583° N, -115.3811° W**
- Bounding box: lat 53.6048 -> 53.7118, lon -115.4895 -> -115.2597
- Located by **Wildwood / Yellowhead County**, west of Edmonton — NOT near 53.39 (that was a memory error, ~29 km too far south).
- Access: Hwy 16 west to Wildwood, then south on the Chip Lake road (Hwy 647 / range roads toward Peers).

## Free Crown land camp points (NORTH / NORTHEAST shore — undeveloped Crown land)
- Point A — North Shore Reed Shelf (best all-rounder): **53.7078, -115.3611**
- Point B — Northeast Arm Cove (sheltered, best perch): **53.7058, -115.2747**
- Point C — North Mid-Shore (open water, biggest pike): **53.7098, -115.3911**
All three confirmed INSIDE the real lake bbox (on water).

## Paid zone to AVOID
- Chip Lake Provincial Recreation Area / lodge is on the **SOUTHWEST shore** — paid campsites. Stay on the north/northeast end.

## Legality check
- Random camping legal on Crown land here (no gate, no "No Camping" post at the north access).
- Verify on **iMap Alberta** (Crown Land + Dispositions layers): green/unmarked at those decimals = legal; pink/leased = shift 400m east along shore.
- **Alberta Sportfishing Licence** required (~$28 resident annual, mywildalberta.ca) — free camping != free license.
- Fish: perch (no limit, 15 over-35cm possession), northern pike (barbless, C&R on breeders), walleye (slot limit — check current regs).
