# Alberta — Crown Land Free Camping & Fishing (verified 2026 session)

## Random camping rule (verified on alberta.ca/camping-on-public-land & /public-lands-camping-pass)
- Dispersed "random camping" on vacant public (Crown) land is **FREE** — no campsite fee, no reservation, first-come first-served.
- **EXCEPTION — must buy a Public Lands Camping Pass** to random camp:
  - Along the **Eastern Slopes of the Rockies** (pass zone runs Grande Prairie → Waterton Lakes NP).
  - **Porcupine Hills PLUZ** and **Willmore Wilderness Park**.
  - On **Agricultural Crown land** within the pass area (needs leaseholder permission anyway).
- Pass cost (2026): **$20 / 3-day**, **$30 / annual** per person 18+. Plus $3.25 processing + GST per transaction, and a one-time **WIN** number ($8) to use AlbertaRELM.
- Serviced campgrounds (PRAs, Provincial Parks): pay nightly fee, NO pass needed there.
- PLUZs exist across the province (18 total: Northern — Athabasca Ranch, Brule Lake, Coal Branch, Holmes Crossing, Whitecourt Sandhills; Central — Blackstone/Wapiabi, Dormer/Sheep, Job/Cline, Kiska/Willson, Panther Corners, Upper Clearwater/Ram; Southern — Cataract Creek, Ghost, Livingstone, McLean Creek, Porcupine Hills, Sibbald). Note the west-central foothills PLUZs sit inside the Eastern Slopes pass zone.

## Fishing licence
- Required year-round EXCEPT during **Free Fishing** periods.
- **2026 Free Fishing: July 6-8, 2026** (Canada Day weekend) — CONFIRMED on alberta.ca/free-fishing-weekend.

## Free-Crown-land lake picks (outside the pass zone = truly free camping)
1. **Lac La Biche** (NE) — top pick. Free Crown-land camping on surrounding public land. Fish: walleye, pike, perch, whitefish. Sights: huge lake, nearby Pinehurst Lake sand dunes, Lakeland PP, Sir Winston Churchill island PP.
2. **Lesser Slave Lake** (N-central) — free Crown land NE of lake. Fish: walleye, pike, whitefish. Sights: Marten Mountain viewpoint, Boreal Centre for Bird Conservation.
3. **Cold Lake** (NE, AB/SK border) — free surrounding Crown land. Fish: walleye, pike, lake trout (deep). Sights: Cold Lake PP cliffs.
4. **Winagami Lake** (N, west of Lesser Slave) — free nearby Crown land. Fish: pike, walleye, perch. Quiet.

## Foothills option (needs $20-30 pass, still no per-night fee)
5. **Hinton / Chip Lake / Athabasca River** — Whitecourt Sandhills, Coal Branch, Brule Lake PLUZs. Fish: Athabasca River goldeye + walleye, Chip Lake pike/perch/trout. Sight: the **"Crooked Trees"** (naturally deformed trees near Hinton).

## Rules to pass on
- Pack out all garbage; no toilets/water/firewood provided — bring or buy locally.
- Don't camp on agricultural lease land without leaseholder OK; don't block lease roads.
- Stay out of the Eastern Slopes pass zone unless holding the pass.
- Check albertarelm.com for slot limits / bait bans per lake before fishing.
